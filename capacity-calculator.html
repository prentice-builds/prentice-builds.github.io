The math is simple on purpose. Capacity returned is the team size multiplied by the change in administrative share, priced at the yearly cost you set. Break-even is the cost of the AI role divided by the cost of the team. A forty hour week is assumed. The dollar figure is capacity, not cash. It turns into money when the team absorbs more work or avoids a hire. None of this is measured data, and the first step for any team is a baseline of where the hours go.

This counts time only. The report's business case names five more returns that this page does not price. Money caught before it is paid out, problems found while they are still cheap, the same standard on every project, experience that stays when people leave, and better work for the people doing it.

How to read the ratings

Value. High is material recurring time or decision exposure. Medium is a narrower or less frequent benefit.

Effort. Low uses approved tools. Medium needs configuration or a focused build. High needs integration or new controls.

Readiness. Available works with today's tools. Validate needs testing on the task. Research is still uncertain at this scope.
