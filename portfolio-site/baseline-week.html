Baseline your week

Before any team decides where AI belongs, it needs to know where the hours go. Log two honest weeks, a typical one and one when monthly reports are due, and this page tells you how much of each is administrative load and how much of that AI could take.

It takes about two minutes at the end of each day. Half hours are close enough.

Running this across a team

Have each project manager log a typical week and a week when monthly reports are due, then download the file or copy the text and send it to whoever is collecting. Paste the files into one spreadsheet and you have a baseline for the team.

Then run it again a few months in. Many meetings exist to move status from one person to another. When reports and logs keep themselves current, some of those meetings should shrink, and a second baseline is how you would see it.

Nothing you enter here is sent anywhere. The hours stay in your own browser until you choose to share them.

The categories come from the report, Where AI belongs in construction project delivery. Repeatable work is what AI can do and you review. Review work is what AI can draft and you decide. Meetings are administrative load that stays yours for now, though AI can take the prep and the follow-up. The rest is the work only you can do.
